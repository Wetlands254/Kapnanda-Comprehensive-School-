# A4 fee documents and leaving certificate update

Fee Statement, Official Fee Structure and Leaving Certificate now share the report-form print standard: centred school identity, larger full Kenya crest, 190 mm content width, A4 portrait page sizing, consistent margins, wrapping tables and print colour preservation. PDF capture uses a matching portrait stage width and 5 mm margins to prevent content extending beyond the A4 printable width.

Fee statements with long payment histories may span additional A4 pages with intact table rows. Fee structure and leaving certificate remain one page for normal-length content.

See `PARENT-NOTIFICATIONS-AND-PRINT-SETUP.md` for automatic fee-payment SMS setup and the current limitation on sending PDF attachments directly to parents.
