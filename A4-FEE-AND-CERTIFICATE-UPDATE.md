# A4 fee documents and leaving certificate update

This update brings the learner fee statement, official fee structure and leaving certificate into the same visual system as the individual learner report form.

- A4 portrait page with print-safe margins.
- Centred school heading, blue divider and gold subtitle matching the report.
- Tables constrained to page width with safe text wrapping.
- Fee statement payment details and totals remain legible.
- Leaving certificate uses a compact one-page layout with a restrained school-colour border and signature area.
- Timetable and whole-school register landscape exports are unchanged.

After uploading, test all three documents using both **Download PDF** and **Print / Preview** on desktop and Android.
